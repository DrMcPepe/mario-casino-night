Master Bartender: The host controls every pour and counts timed drinks aloud. Penalties called while the host is driving are served at the next safe controller handoff or race finish.

Phase 1: Beerio Kart Relay

  • Mario Kart Wii VS Mode, exactly three races.
  • Four teams sized 3 / 3 / 3 / 4. Each kart fields three active racers.

  • Rolling, coasting, drifting, or moving under Lakitu while sipping: one penalty shot.
  • After the finish, more than 2 oz combined across the lineup's three cans: all three active racers receive two penalty shots.

Phase 2: Casino

  • Starting wallet 15 seconds. Wallet maximum 60 seconds.
  • A lost bet becomes the same number of self-incurred drinking seconds.
  • Only credited betting profit can be assigned to another player.
  • Each player can receive at most 20 assigned seconds for the entire event.
  • Self-losses, Beerio drinks, and buy-back shots do not count toward that 20-second cap.
  • At a zero wallet, one bartender-confirmed buy-back shot adds 5 wallet seconds.
  • Penalty-themed props have no extra shot. The lost stake is the entire penalty.
Stop rule: The host may pause play or refuse service at any time. A participant may stop participating without punishment. No one drives after participating.

Casino Rulebook

Match Formats

Tennis, three-frame Bowling, one-round Boxing, and one-hole Golf. All wagers close before gameplay begins.

Betting

  • The host opens a 30- or 45-second window. The server clock is final.
  • Players can wager up to their current wallet and can back only one selection per market.
  • Players may undo all stakes in a market while betting remains open. Bets become final at lock.
  • Odds show profit. A 5-second winner at 2:1 returns the 5-second stake plus 10 seconds profit.
  • Odds of 1.5:1 require an even stake.
  • Wallet payouts stop at 60. Overflow is discarded.
  • Voided markets return their stakes. Losing wagers enter the bettor's unpaid tab.

Assignments and Buy-Backs

  • Assignments spend the winner's credited profit and create an equal recipient tab.
  • A recipient at 20 lifetime assigned seconds is permanently greyed out.
  • Serving an assignment does not reopen target capacity.
  • A zero-wallet player requests a buy-back. The host serves one shot, confirms it, and the server adds 5 base seconds.

Service Rules

  • The host is the sole bartender.
  • Use measured pours for shots. Timed seconds are counted aloud.
  • Do not clear a ledger entry until it is served.
  • Do not override the 20-second assignment defense.
  • Self-incurred losses have no software cap, but the host always retains stop-service authority.
  • No non-alcoholic gameplay substitution is part of these event rules.
