# Event Runbook

## Preflight

- Arrange rides before drinking begins.
- Set out food, water, measured shot service, towels, and waste containers.
- Connect the PC to the TV as an extended display.
- Test Dolphin, all four controllers, Mario Kart Wii, and each Wii Sports mode.
- Start the server and test the QR code from a phone on Wi-Fi.
- Import all 13 names and have everyone claim their own profile.
- Test one practice wager, then replace the roster to reset the event.
- Open OBS scenes and confirm that Beerio has no browser overlay.
- Print `/rules` and fill in the Beerio roster and referee draw.

## Beerio

Use the printed sheet. The app and TV sportsbook are not needed during this phase. The host starts as race-one referee, then joins races two and three through the utility substitution rotation.

## Casino Card

A practical seven-match card gives every player at least one appearance. Put twelve players into six unique 1v1 matches, then let the thirteenth player challenge one returning participant in match seven. Mix the four sports across the card.

For every match:

1. Build the match and verify names, sport, and odds.
2. Switch OBS to Casino Dashboard.
3. Open betting for 30 or 45 seconds.
4. Let the server lock the book.
5. Switch OBS to Casino Game and click Start.
6. Record every outcome after play finishes.
7. Review all outcome selectors, then resolve once.
8. Serve the ledger queue and confirm buy-backs.

## Close

- Stop opening new matches.
- Serve or explicitly cancel remaining service according to host judgment.
- Confirm transportation for every participant.
- Stop the server with `Ctrl+C`.
- Keep `data/event-state.json` if event history is needed, or delete it before the next event.
